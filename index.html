Privacy by design

Your footage. Your rules.

A security system should protect your privacy as carefully as your property. These four commitments are built into the system from day one, not tucked into a settings menu.

AI runs in your home, not our cloud.

Threat verification happens on the Hub. Footage is encrypted in transit and at rest, and monitoring agents see a live feed only during a verified event.

Police get what they need, when you say so.

One tap calls 911, and your address, a live-feed link and an evidence package are ready for responding officers. Otherwise your footage is released only by you or under a lawful order.

Bedrooms are off‑limits by default.

Guardian’s no-go zones are enforced on the device, and its camera shutter is physical and visible.

Neighbors stay out of frame.

Sentinel never crosses your property line, and anything beyond it is blurred at capture.

Where and when can I get AeroHome?

AeroHome launches in 2026 as a pilot program in Phoenix and Dallas–Fort Worth, limited to the first 500 homes. Join the waitlist to hold a place. If you live elsewhere, choose “Elsewhere, notify me” and we’ll tell you when AeroHome reaches your area.

What does it cost?

Hardware is $999 for the Guardian Kit, $1,499 for the Sentinel Kit or $2,499 for AeroHome Complete, and any kit can be financed over 36 months from about $28 a month. Plans are $49, $89 or $149 a month. Use Build your setup to see a first-year total.

Will it wake me up every time a raccoon crosses the yard?

No. The Hub’s on-device AI sorts every detection into person, vehicle, animal or package. You’re alerted when a real person is verified, not for every motion event.

Can I share footage with police?

Yes. One tap calls 911, and your address, a live-feed link and a documented 4K and thermal evidence package are ready for the officers who respond. The Total plan adds video-verified alarm reporting, which many police departments prioritize. Otherwise, your footage is released only by you or under a lawful order.

Does the drone fly over my neighbors?

No. Sentinel is geofenced to your property and never crosses your property line. Anything the camera sees beyond it is blurred at capture.

What happens if someone cuts the power or the internet?

The Hub has cellular and battery backup and records locally, so cutting the power or the internet line keeps the system running on backup.

Will the robot go into bedrooms?

Not by default. Bedrooms are no-go zones enforced on the device, and Guardian’s camera has a physical shutter you can see.

Will it lower my home-insurance premium?

It may. Many insurers discount premiums 5 to 15% for verified, monitored systems, and the Total plan includes 24/7 professional monitoring and an insurance documentation pack. Check with your carrier for your exact discount.

What do waitlist members get?

Priority installation, founder pricing and 60 days of the Total plan free. Joining the waitlist is free and doesn’t commit you to buy.
